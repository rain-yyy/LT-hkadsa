<h2 class="center">Organizing and Training AD team to Provide Audio Description Services for Art Gallery</h2>

<img src="/images/activities/september-2025-2/1.jpg" style="width: 100%; padding: 5px;">

<p style="text-indent: 30px;">
  On 5 September 2025, the Hong Kong Audio Description Studies Association (HKADSA) trained an AD service team to provide AD services for the Art Gallery at City University of Hong Kong. The initiative focused on equipping team members with the skills needed to deliver accessible art experiences and supporting visitors with visual impairments through effective communication and well-prepared service procedures.
</p>
